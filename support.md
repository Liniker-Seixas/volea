# Support

<p class="lede">Most answers are below. For anything else, open an issue and we will reply there.</p>

<p class="note"><strong>Get help:</strong> <a href="https://github.com/Liniker-Seixas/volea/issues/new">open an issue on GitHub</a>. Issues are public, so describe the problem and your iPhone, Apple Watch and their software versions, but never post health data, locations or other players' names.</p>

## Shots aren't being detected

Start a match on the watch (Start Match) and wear it snugly on your hitting arm. Check your hitting hand in Volea's Settings on the iPhone, and try a higher detection sensitivity there if soft touch shots are missed. Settings sync to the watch by themselves.

## A match didn't appear on my iPhone

The watch sends each session to the iPhone when it ends, over Apple's Watch Connectivity link. Keep both nearby with Bluetooth on, and open Volea on the iPhone; a session waiting on the watch arrives within a minute or so.

## Where do I put the iPhone for Court Cam?

Behind a back glass, high and central, in landscape, so the whole court is in frame. Then mark the four court landmarks Volea asks for and drag them onto the lines. No court handy? Tap "Watch a demo match" on Court Cam's first screen.

## Court Cam says "The camera moved"

Bump guard noticed that the phone was knocked to a new angle, which would shift every position on the court. Drag the markers back onto the court lines and tap Resume. You can switch Bump guard off in Settings, Court Cam, Court IQ.

## Court IQ shows no insights about my pair

Pair and opponent insights need at least a minute of play with both players of a pair tracked. Rallies and speed show sooner.

## There is no heart-rate data

Heart rate comes from your Apple Watch during a match. iOS never tells apps what you chose for Health access, so check it in the Health app: your profile, Apps, Volea.

## How does Siri scoring work?

During a match on the watch, say "Hey Siri, Volea point us", "Volea point them" or "Undo point in Volea". It works from the watch face too.

## Does Volea send my data anywhere?

No. Your shots, heart rate, court positions and scores stay on your iPhone and Apple Watch, and move between them only over Apple's encrypted device-to-device link. Court Cam never records video. Details are in the [privacy policy]({{ site.baseurl }}/privacy/).

## How do I delete my data?

Settings, Data, Delete all sessions removes every session and its Court Cam recording. Deleting the app removes everything else. Workouts already saved to Apple Health stay under your control in the Health app.

## Is Volea a medical device?

No. Volea is a sports tracker: its heart rate, calorie and fitness figures are for training, never for health decisions. Talk to a doctor about any health concern.
