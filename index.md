# Your Apple Watch becomes a padel coach.

<p class="lede">Volea detects every shot from your wrist (forehands, backhands, volleys, smashes, bandejas, lobs and serves), keeps the score with real padel rules, and shows you what to work on next.</p>

On the iPhone, Court Cam films a match from behind the back glass and maps all four players, and Court IQ reads the match: rallies, how you and your partner move as a pair, the opponents' habits and your speed. Every match is saved to Apple Health as a workout.

There is no server and no account. Your data stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch. Volea is in beta on TestFlight.
